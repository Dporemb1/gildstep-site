Why I built it

I never stuck to my goals.

I kept them in my head, and writing out a full step-by-step plan felt like a chore I'd quit before I started. Then I read The ONE Thing by Gary Keller, and one idea clicked: take a big goal and break it down, again and again, until what's left is small enough to do today. So I built the app I wanted: make the plan once, then show up day by day and watch it add up. The first goal I set in it was finishing the app itself.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Free covers two active goals with full plans, your first AI plan, today's One Thing, check-ins, streaks, progress and the Feed. Premium adds the daily AI coach, plans for every goal, full monthly reports and focus without limits.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. Gildstep is iPhone only for now.

How does the AI plan work?

You answer a few questions: the goal, how you'll track it, your finish line and the time you can give each week. Gildstep writes a checkpoint for every month and the steps for your first two weeks, usually in under a minute. You review every line before it joins your plan, and each new week is planned in a short weekly reset.

What happens to my data?

Your goals, check-ins and One Things are private to you. Other people only see what you choose to share to the Feed and your profile card. Nothing is sold or used for ads, and you can export everything or delete your account from the app. The details are in the privacy policy.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Premium is a subscription through Apple, so you cancel in your iPhone's Settings and keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. Everything in Free stays free.
